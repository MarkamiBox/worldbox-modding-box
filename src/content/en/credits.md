## Modding Platforms & Core Frameworks

  - Special thanks to the NML maintainers and core contributors who maintain the mod loader.
- **[BepInEx](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx)**
  - The Unity/XNA modding framework and plugin architecture, even though I don't use them much.
- **[Harmony](https://github.com/pardeike/Harmony)**
  - Created by **Andreas Pardeike**. The powerful library for patching, hooking, and replacing .NET methods at runtime without altering game files on disk. 

---

## Investigation & Analysis Tools

- **[UnityExplorer](https://github.com/sinai-dev/UnityExplorer)**
  - Created by **sinai-dev**. The in-game inspector.
- **[dnSpy](https://github.com/dnSpy/dnSpy) & [ILSpy](https://github.com/icsharpcode/ILSpy)**
  - The decompiler and debugger that lets us read the game's compiled `Assembly-CSharp.dll` to understand mechanics and discover internal methods.
- **[AssetRipper](https://github.com/AssetRipper/AssetRipper)**
  - An open-source tool for inspecting and extracting sprites, textures, fonts, and sound effects from Unity asset bundles and serialized files.
