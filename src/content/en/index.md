# Introduction :wbhi:

> [!NOTE] A small disclaimer
> Hello  :3718-litol-shy: , I'm Markami. I've been modding this game for over 5 years and in all of this time I would rate myself as an average modder :pain:, even though, I would like to try make a cool guide for modding in this game. It will not be a serious guide as there are others for that, but it will be a mix between cool stuff and funny jokes. Enjoy :ez_pepe:.

## How it is organized

- **Overview**: This introduction, what you need to get started, troubleshooting, external tools (BepInEx, UnityExplorer, dnSpy, AssetRipper), BepInEx modding, and community credits & feedback.
- **NML Modding**: Everything about the loader and the mod pipeline - starting with **[Installing NML](#/install-nml)** (start there if you only want to *play* with mods):
  - **Core Workflow**: Mod structure, your first mod, logs & debugging, localization, and custom sprites.
  - **Advanced & Publishing**: In-game settings menus, Harmony patches, saving data in saves, and publishing to Steam Workshop.
- **Game Content**: The actual content you add to the world, organized into 7 clear categories:
  - **Architecture & Stats**: How asset libraries work, stats reference, and the asset index.
  - **Traits & Genetics**: Unit traits, trait groups, subspecies traits & genes, culture, religion, clans, languages, and kingdom traits.
  - **Items & Equipment**: Custom weapons & armor, item modifiers & materials, item groups, and drops & loot.
  - **Actors, Buildings & AI**: Custom creatures, buildings, custom AI behaviours & city jobs, status effects, and projectiles & spells.
  - **World & Civilizations**: Custom tiles, biome clouds, world ages, world laws, kingdoms & diplomacy, plots & rebellions, resources, and disasters.
  - **God Powers & UI**: God powers & brushes, power buttons, and custom windows with tabs & tooltips.
  - **Finishing Touches**: In-game achievements and putting everything together into a finished mod.
- **Modding Tools**: Interactive utilities built directly into this guide (Harmony Patch Builder, Asset Fields explorer, Method Finder, and Discord Icon Finder).

Read it top to bottom the first time. After that, jump around like everybody else does :PES2_Shrug:.
