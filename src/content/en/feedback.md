# Feedback & requests :wbdiplomacyhandshake:

This guide is written by one person (and an army of AIs  :PES3_Shhh:), so half of what is missing is stuff I simply forgot about  :304977-pepehang:. If something here is wrong, unclear, or just not there, tell me.

## Send it

Pick what it is about, write it, and the button opens a **prefilled GitHub issue** in a new tab.

::tool:feedback::

## What makes a good report

Three lines beat three paragraphs:

1. **What you did** - "I followed Custom traits and copied the code block."
2. **What you expected** - "The trait shows up in the unit window."
3. **What happened instead** - "Nothing, and the log says `NullReferenceException ... HelloTraits.cs:24`."

If you have a log line, paste it. One real log line is worth ten sentences of description  :PES5_NiceThumbsUp: 

> [!TIP] Check the log before reporting
> Half of the "it does not work" reports are answered by the log, and you get your answer in thirty seconds instead of waiting for me. **[Logs & debugging](#/nml/logs-and-debugging)** shows you where it is and how to read it, and **[Troubleshooting](#/troubleshooting)** lists the errors people hit most.
